02 / 01 / IDENTITY

A perspective shaped by complementary businesses.

Blue Line Capital Group describes its business interests across finance, foreign exchange, real estate, construction, industrial manufacturing, importation, assembly and trade. Financial administration, people operations and digital systems help connect these activities at group level.

The Group is presented as headquartered in Douala, Cameroon. This corporate website is designed to communicate its areas of activity and strategic direction without making unsupported claims about legal entities, financial performance, licences or completed projects.

03 / 02 / STRUCTURE

A connected structure, not a one-size-fits-all business.
