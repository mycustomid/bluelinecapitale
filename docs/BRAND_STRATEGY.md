# Creative direction — Blue Line Capital Group

**Working concept:** Independent thinking. Connected enterprise.

## Brand positioning

The company should present as a *multi-sector enterprise group* rooted in Cameroon rather than a generic private equity site or a vaguely global investment fund. Nine portfolio entries in the original repo mix businesses and shared group functions. We call these **areas of activity** and avoid asserting that all nine are separate incorporated subsidiaries.

**Audiences:** potential trading partners, landlords and commercial property counterparties, investors and professional collaborators, potential talent, and institutional stakeholders. Most need clarity about *what each activity actually does*, how to enquire, and whether the claims can be trusted.

## Colour psychology (creative rationale, not a scientific prediction)

- `#0b2030` / deep petrol navy: sober, stable, enterprise-scale background.
- `#f4f1e9` / warm alabaster: readable, confident editorial counterweight; avoids relentless dark SaaS styling.
- `#c6a16d` / brushed muted gold: restrained signal for key actions and hierarchy, not glitter or a luxury-gimmick gradient.
- Grey-blue body text: supports longer form content without overwhelming contrast.

These are design hypotheses. They are **not** empirical proof that visitors will invest more because a colour was used.

## Editorial hierarchy

Large typographic proposition → clear sector context → precise operational capabilities → transparent FAQs → actionable contact. No invented statistics, awards, revenue growth, project galleries, or fake testimonial names. The homepage does not promise liquidity, audited performance, active industrial plants or regulated authorisations.

## Layout composition

1. Atmospheric architectural hero, deliberately restrained, no animated counters or carousel overload.
2. Editorial asymmetry and generous breathing room.
3. Sector photography and local offline fallback visuals, each marked illustrative.
4. Contrast of deep-toned full-width imagery and warm content panels.
5. Semantic links, native FAQ disclosure, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.

## Content decisions

Business content developed independently for exchange, property, construction, sugar industry, trading, finance, people and digital. The copy explains possible operating responsibilities based on the original repo, while making clear where actual regulatory status, projects and commercial availability require validation. EN/FR are separate rendered HTML pages with proper `lang` attributes and SEO hreflang.
